# ADR: UI pass 4 decisions (2026-10-23, owner)

1. Accent: the single UI accent is the existing teal (`--pen` token). The earlier "ink blue" wording (pass 2 and 3 briefs and reviewer rubric) is retired; the accent is called "teal ink". No accent token change.
2. Pen orange stays only on the pen drawing. The violet brave series from `app/src/charts/palette.ts` stays.
3. DONE is no longer "both reviewers score every line at 4 and a mean of 4.3". Nine review cycles stayed between 3.3 and 3.55 and reviewers read 50 % cropped contact sheets. DONE is now the objective list in BRIEF4 section 8 (gates, budgets, axe, reduced motion, forced colors, one full-size review whose "blocking at real size" items are fixed or logged). Reviewer scores are still collected and reported; they do not gate DONE.
4. Pass 4 is authorized for exactly one review cycle, plus fixes for defects the reviewers verify at real size.
